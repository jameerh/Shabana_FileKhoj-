# Shabana FileKhoj branding

Product: **Shabana FileKhoj**  
Marathi tagline: **तुमचे सर्व दस्तऐवज, शोधा क्षणार्धात!**  
English tagline: **Save once. Search anything. Find instantly.**

## Visual direction

- Primary brand: deep emerald green with warm metallic gold highlights.
- Home screen: a high-contrast green hero panel, clear Marathi promise, English supporting tagline and a search-first layout.
- Launcher icon: `app/src/main/res/drawable/filekhoj_app_icon.xml` — simplified file-and-search mark for legibility at small sizes.
- The supplied campaign artwork is a promotional composition with mascot, devices and feature callouts. Use it in promotional material, not as a full-size launcher icon; retain its identity and artwork without distortion.
- Keep app UI assets lightweight and bundled. Do not make the launcher or home screen dependent on a remote image download.

## Tone

Helpful, fast, private and familiar to document-service users. Prefer Marathi for the product promise and simple English labels for common controls, while keeping important actions explicit.
